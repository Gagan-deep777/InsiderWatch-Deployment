NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE & INFORMATION SECURITY POLICIES
Document ID: NXT-POL-010
Version: 2.1
Classification: BASIC | Internal
Effective date: 01 October 2026
Policy owner: Business Operations

1. PURPOSE
These policies define the minimum expectations for using company systems, handling information and protecting colleagues, customers and business operations.

2. ACCEPTABLE USE
• Use company accounts and devices for authorised work.
• Do not install unapproved software or disable endpoint protections.
• Lock your screen whenever you leave your workstation.
• Do not bypass access controls or attempt to access another employee's account.
• Report lost devices, suspicious messages and unexpected access prompts promptly.

3. IDENTITY AND ACCESS
• Every employee must use an individual account; shared accounts are prohibited.
• Passwords must not be shared, reused or stored in plain-text documents.
• Multi-factor authentication must be enabled wherever supported.
• Access is granted using least privilege and must be reviewed when duties change.
• Managers must request access changes through the approved IT process.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION
BASIC: General company information and approved internal guidance.
MEDIUM: Internal project, operational and technical information.
CONFIDENTIAL: Financial, security, customer-sensitive and restricted business records.
Employees must use approved storage and sharing channels appropriate to the classification.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING
Report suspected phishing, malware, data loss, unauthorised access or account compromise to security@nexora.example. Include the time, affected system and a short description. Do not delete relevant evidence or investigate beyond your authorisation.

6. REMOTE WORK
Use approved secure connectivity. Avoid public or shared computers. Do not leave company devices unattended in public places. Report remote-access failures to IT rather than disabling security controls.

7. RECORDS AND RETENTION
Operational and security events are logged by the InsiderWatch demonstration system. The live File Access view displays the latest 24 hours; older event records are archived in the history database. Retention and deletion in a production company must follow its approved legal and records-management policy.

8. POLICY BREACHES
Suspected breaches are reviewed by the designated administrator and relevant manager. Temporary account restrictions may be applied while facts are established. Restoration requires an authorised decision and a recorded outcome.

Approval: Demonstration policy approved by Nexora Operations.
Review cycle: Every 12 months or after a material security change.

All company names, policy owners, dates and processes here are illustrative demo content.
